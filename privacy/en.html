SparkBuild · Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: November 9, 2026

SparkBuild is developed and operated by JQ-Miao. This policy explains how SparkBuild handles information when you create and run personal tools. Contact: sparkbuild2026@outlook.com. No SparkBuild account is required. You can read this policy offline in Settings.

Local information and API keys

Projects, source code, development conversations, revisions, tool records and selected media are stored on your device. Preferences are saved in system settings storage. AI API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain and sent to the selected AI endpoint to authenticate requests. The host does not expose its AI keys to tool pages.

SparkBuild has no built-in cloud sync. iOS backups and files you export may contain local data, depending on your system settings and choices.

Information sent to AI services

Before the first content request, SparkBuild shows the receiving service address, data categories and purpose and asks for explicit consent. Creation conversations and each individual project have separate consent. Existing general AI permissions do not replace it.

When you send a creation, editing or repair request, SparkBuild sends your instructions, relevant conversation history, project information, source code, tool results and selected images as needed to the configured AI endpoint. Repair context can include runtime errors and diagnostics. When you allow a tool to use AI, it can send its chosen messages and images through that endpoint. These inputs may contain personal information you provide. Chat images are re-encoded to remove original EXIF metadata, including GPS tags; visible content can still identify people or places.

Supported providers include DeepSeek, Anthropic/Claude, OpenAI, Google/Gemini and Cursor. A custom base URL sends requests and credentials to that configured server or proxy. The built-in AI request path uses DeepSeek and includes a random per-install identifier for request separation and abuse prevention. This is not an advertising identifier. Each endpoint also receives ordinary network metadata, such as your IP address.

Your choice applies to this service address and use. A different service address requires new consent. Revoke consent in Settings → AI Settings → AI data sharing. Revocation stops further requests; it cannot recall data already sent.

System permissions and sensitive information

Only use permissions you need for a tool. Camera, microphone, selected photos, files, selected contacts and reminders use system interfaces and applicable authorization. Saving to Photos requests add-only access. Calendar addition opens the system editor for your confirmation; SparkBuild does not request calendar read access or provide calendar listing, editing or deletion. This version does not provide health, motion or location access. Revoking access does not erase information already saved.

OCR and image analysis use Apple Vision locally. Translation uses Apple’s on-device translation with language downloads when needed. Speech recognition runs on-device when supported; otherwise Apple may process audio online. Face ID and Touch ID return an authentication result; SparkBuild does not receive biometric templates. Review a tool’s behavior before sharing sensitive information.

Widgets, exports and external content

Widgets and Live Activities use the card content you choose. The app and its extension share the necessary data through an iOS App Group. Content can appear on the Home Screen or Lock Screen; consider visibility before adding sensitive information.

Source ZIP exports include code, project description/design memory and your chosen icon, including a cropped photo. They exclude tool records, saved tool media, permissions, development chats and revision history. Other export, share or print actions can include the content you choose. Tools can load external resources, call services or open links; those destinations receive the corresponding requests and any data the tool sends.

Network requests and diagnostics

The app fetches version and configuration information from Tencent Cloud COS. Its host receives ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses; this configuration request does not include your project source or conversations. External dependency checks also contact the relevant resource hosts.

SparkBuild does not integrate advertising, third-party analytics or automatic third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. Local operational logs may record errors, provider/model details and request statistics. Development builds can also print request and response content to the development console. Diagnostics are not automatically sent to the developer; information you submit through AI repair or support is sent to the corresponding recipient.

Optional support purchases

If you choose to support the developer with a coffee, Apple processes the in-app payment. SparkBuild uses StoreKit product and transaction information on your device to display local prices, verify purchases and finish transactions. The app does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account credentials and does not upload purchase records to the developer’s server. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy. Support is optional, does not unlock features or AI credits, and is not a subscription.

Recipients, processing regions and retention by providers

Information leaves your device when required for an AI request, a system online service, external tool content, an export you choose or support email. AI providers, configured servers/proxies, Apple, resource hosts and email providers handle the information they receive under their own terms and privacy policies. Processing may take place outside your country or region.

Provider retention, security, subcontractors and possible use for model training depend on the service, account settings and agreement. SparkBuild cannot promise zero retention or no training for every endpoint. Check the relevant provider’s terms before sending personal or confidential information. SparkBuild does not automatically send your complete photo library, address book or all projects to these services.

Retention and deletion

Local information is generally kept until you delete it. Moving a project to Trash retains its data; permanently deleting it removes its local workspace, records, media, development attachments and associated web storage. Calendar events and reminders already written to your system accounts remain yours to manage in Calendar or Reminders. Previously exported or shared copies and backups require separate deletion.

Remove saved AI credentials through AI settings before uninstalling if needed: iOS Keychain items may survive uninstalling. Uninstalling is not a request to erase copies already held by an AI provider or email service. Their retention rules apply; request deletion from the relevant provider. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations, and deleted when no longer needed.

Your choices, requests and support

You can view and edit local content, export source, permanently delete projects, change AI endpoints, remove saved credentials and revoke permissions. AI and optional permissions are not required to read this policy or access existing local content that does not depend on them.

If you email us, JQ-Miao receives your email address, any name your email client includes, your message and attachments you choose. We use these to respond, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. Do not include API keys or unnecessary sensitive information. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent, and complain to a competent authority. Contact sparkbuild2026@outlook.com; we will respond under applicable law and may reasonably verify your identity. We can handle information we control; third-party copies may require a request to their provider.

Your choice applies to this service address and use. A different service address requires new consent. Revoke consent in Settings → AI Settings → AI data sharing. Revocation stops further requests; it cannot recall data already sent.

Security

SparkBuild uses the iOS sandbox, Keychain, system permission controls and HTTPS for its built-in network endpoints to help protect information. Tool storage is separated by project. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. A custom endpoint or imported/generated tool may have different security characteristics; review what you run, authorize and share.

Children and minors

SparkBuild is not designed specifically for children. Minors should use it with appropriate guardian guidance and comply with the age rules of any connected service. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer without required authorization, contact us so we can review and delete information under our control as required by law.

Policy changes

We update this policy when functionality or data handling changes. The current policy and its effective date are available in Settings. Where applicable law requires notice or consent for a material change, we will provide that notice or obtain the necessary consent.
