SparkBuild Support

SparkBuild is developed by JQ-Miao. It helps you create, edit, and run personal mini apps on iPhone and iPad through conversation. You do not need a SparkBuild account.

Contact

For help, feedback, or privacy requests, email sparkbuild2026@outlook.com.

Please do not include API keys or other unnecessary sensitive information in your message.

FAQ

Do coffee tips unlock features?

No. Tips are optional one-time support. They do not unlock features or AI credits, and they are not a subscription.

Where is my data stored?

Projects and conversations stay on your device by default. Information leaves the device only when you use AI, export content, or email support. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I revoke permissions or delete data?

You can revoke a project's capabilities in its settings, or delete the project. The privacy policy explains this in more detail.
